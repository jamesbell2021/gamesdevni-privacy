# Privacy Policy — Little World Railways

*Last updated: 8 November 2026*

Little World Railways is a game made by **3BL_Indie** (Northern Ireland).

## The short version

Little World Railways does not collect, store or send any personal information to us or to anyone else. It is a single-player game with no accounts, no adverts, no analytics or tracking, and no online features. It does not connect to the internet.

## What stays on your PC

- **Saved railways and settings** (volume, graphics, music and festive-season choices) are saved in the game's own data folder on your PC. They are never uploaded, and they are removed when you uninstall the game.

## Links

The free demo has a "Get the full version" button. If you click it, the game's itch.io page opens in your web browser, and that website's own privacy policy applies. The full game has no web links.

## Microsoft Store

If you bought the game from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the purchase and download under its own privacy statement (<https://privacy.microsoft.com>). We receive only the anonymous sales and crash summaries that Microsoft provides to every developer.

## Children

The game does not ask for or collect any information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
