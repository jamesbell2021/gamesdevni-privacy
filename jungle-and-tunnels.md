# Privacy Policy — Jungle & Tunnels

*Last updated: 18 November 2026*

Jungle & Tunnels is a game made by **3BL_Indie** (Northern Ireland).

## The short version

Jungle & Tunnels does not collect, store or send any personal information to us or to anyone else. It has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics or tracking.

## What stays on your PC

- **Settings, campaign progress and battlefields you make in the Battlefield Editor** are saved in the game's own data folder on your PC. They are never uploaded.

## The Microsoft Store version

If you buy the game from the Microsoft Store, the purchase is made in the Store under Microsoft's own privacy policy. The game itself does not check or report anything about your purchase, and nothing is sent to us.

## Multiplayer

Two-player games and LAN Battle (up to 20 players, 10 a side) send game moves directly between the players' PCs. The hosting PC runs the battle. Nothing goes through our servers (we don't have any), and there is no chat.

- **On the same network**, a hosting PC announces its game on the local network so the other PCs can find it. The announcement includes the player name typed in the game (you can use any name). In a LAN Battle, every player's name is shown to the others in that game.
- **Over the internet**, the joining player types the host's address. To make this possible, the host's PC asks the home router (using UPnP) to open one network port for the game while it is hosting. The other player's PC can see the host's internet address, as with any direct connection.

None of this data is sent to us. Windows may ask whether to allow the game through its firewall the first time you play with other PCs.

## Links

The free demo has a link to the full game's page. If you click it, it opens in your web browser, and that website's own privacy policy applies.

## Children

The game does not ask for or collect any information from anyone, including children.
