# Privacy Policy — Bolts & Boulders

*Last updated: 7 November 2026*

Bolts & Boulders is a game made by **3BL_Indie** (Northern Ireland).

## The short version

Bolts & Boulders does not collect, store or send any personal information to us or to anyone else. It has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics or tracking.

## What stays on your PC

- **Saved progress, settings and castles you design in the Castle Designer** are saved in the game's own data folder on your PC. They are never uploaded.
- **Screenshots and replay GIFs** are saved to your Pictures folder only when you choose to save one.

## Multiplayer

LAN multiplayer (battles and sieges between two PCs) sends game moves directly between the players' PCs on the same local network. The game also broadcasts on the local network so a host can be found. This data is not sent to us or over the internet. Windows may ask whether to allow the game through its firewall the first time you use LAN play.

## Links

The game has a link to our web page. If you click it, it opens in your web browser, and that website's own privacy policy applies.

## Children

The game does not ask for or collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
