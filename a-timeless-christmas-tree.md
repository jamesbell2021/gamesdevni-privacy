# Privacy Policy — A Timeless Christmas Tree

*Last updated: 28 November 2026*

A Timeless Christmas Tree is a free game made by **3BL_Indie** (Northern Ireland).

## The short version

A Timeless Christmas Tree does not collect, store or send any personal information to us or to anyone else. It has no accounts, no adverts, no purchases and no analytics or tracking, and it works without an internet connection.

## What stays on your PC

- **Your trees, album pictures, progress and settings** are saved in the game's own data folder on your PC. They are never uploaded.
- **Christmas cards** made with *Share Your Design* are saved to your Pictures folder only when you choose to save one. A name you type on a card appears only on that picture. Sending the picture to anyone is up to you.

## Links

The game has a button that opens our website, gamesdevni.site. It does nothing unless you press it. The website opens in your web browser, and the privacy practices of that website and the services it links to then apply.

## Children

The game does not ask for or collect any information from anyone, including children.
