# Privacy Policy — The House Is Haunted

*Last updated: 28 November 2026*

The House Is Haunted is a game made by **3BL_Indie** (Northern Ireland).

## The short version

The House Is Haunted does not collect, store or send any personal information to us or to anyone else. It has no accounts, no adverts, no in-game purchases and no analytics or tracking, and it plays without an internet connection.

## What stays on your PC

- **Saved games, settings, Free Play best times and any residents you make yourself** (including the names you give them) are kept in the game's own data folder on your PC. They are never uploaded.

## Buying the game

If you buy the game from a store, that store handles your payment under its own privacy policy. The game itself never sees your payment details.

## Links

The game has a button that opens our website, gamesdevni.site. It does nothing unless you press it. The website opens in your web browser, and the privacy practices of that website and the services it links to then apply.

## Children

The game does not ask for or collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
