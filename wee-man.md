# Privacy Policy — Wee Man

*Last updated: 18 November 2026*

Wee Man is a game made by **3BL_Indie** (Northern Ireland).

## The short version

Wee Man does not collect, store or send any personal information to us or to anyone else. It has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics or tracking.

## His AI and his voice run on your PC

Wee Man's words are written by an AI model, and spoken by a voice program, that both run **on your own computer**. They use only your PC's own local address (127.0.0.1). Nothing you type to him, and nothing he says, is sent over the internet, to us or to anyone else.

## What stays on your PC

His saves (his memories, what you've said to him, his notebook, photos he takes, his settings and the record of past Wee Men) are kept in the game's own data folder on your PC. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

## Neighbours (other PCs on your home network)

If another PC on the **same home network** is running Wee Man, the two games find each other with a small announcement on the local network and exchange game messages directly: postcards, presents, party visits, his name and his look. This never leaves your home network and nothing goes through our servers (we don't have any). Windows may ask whether to let the game through its firewall.

## Reporting what he said

**Settings > Report what he said** opens your own email program with a new message to us containing his last reply. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it. If you do, we receive your email address and what you wrote, and use it only to look into the report and reply to you.

## Links

Some screens have links to our website. If you click one, it opens in your web browser, and that website's own privacy policy applies.

## Children

The game does not ask for or collect any information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
