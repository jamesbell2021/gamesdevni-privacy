# Privacy Policy: Planks & Powder

**Last updated: 17 November 2026**

Planks & Powder is a game by 3BL_Indie (James Bell, Northern Ireland).

## What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, store or send any personal information.

## What stays on your PC

The game saves these files in your own user folder on your PC:

- your settings (sound, graphics, controls)
- the ships you design
- your voyage progress
- your Open Sea high scores, with the names you type in

These files never leave your PC. Removing the game's data folder deletes them.

## Network

The game does not use the internet. If you choose **LAN Battle**, the game talks only to other PCs on the same local network, to find and play that match. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

## Accounts, adverts and purchases

There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no in-game purchases.

## Children

The game is suitable for families. It collects no information from anyone, including children.
